One front page for one day: Tuesday 25 November 2026 — the whole day in 37 stories.

THE CAPITAL STANDARD is an original one-page newspaper drawn in ink blue and
steel grey. Its lead trio is the day itself: drone strikes in Gaza that killed
seven people including a senior Hamas security official; Iran's claim — labelled
as a claim — that three tankers were hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz; and
NATO's plan to send AWACS early-warning jets to Poland after Russian missiles
landed there, with Chancellor Merz pledging Germany's complete support.

Thirteen full-scene cards carry the day's biggest stories, each with a layered
flat illustration, a two-fact deck, a data chip and a source-and-certainty tag.
Twenty-four numbered briefs complete the count of 37 stories across nine
regions: Europe, the Middle East, Africa, the Americas, Asia, South Asia,
Asia-Pacific, Oceania and world stories. A numbered dotted map ties its pins to
the card numbers, a timeline orders the day, and a date-check strip names the
older stories that are not Tuesday's news.

Numbers come only from the day's reporting: 7 killed in Gaza, 3 tankers in the
Iranian claim, more than 700,000 Russian dead in a new analysis, 50 Boeing
737-8 jets ordered by Xiamen Airlines, Madrid's 5-1 comeback over PSV, five
Lynx KF41 vehicles reaching Ukraine, and Nvidia's +31.5% Monday close, tagged
honestly as a Monday close. The US PCE report is shown as scheduled and due
today, with no invented figure.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program uses only the Python standard library. It copies the embedded
`entry.png` (exactly 1024x1024) to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, every
run. `compose.py` is the local drawing script that produced the image.
